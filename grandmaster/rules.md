# Rules: Jack's AI Grandmaster challenge

Summary of Jack's Skool post in the Updates category, read Oct 2, 2026, about 12:15 PM ET. Jack says more details are coming, so check the post again before you submit.

## Task
Recreate or improve one app that people pay for. Jack gives Granola as an example.

## Judging
- Effectiveness.
- Looks.
- Creativity.
- Simplicity.
- Value.

A bigger problem, solved more elegantly, gets a higher score.

## Submission
1. Make a post in the **Dubai Grandmaster** category (pinned comment). The main post now sits under "AI Grandmaster Competition"; confirm the category name before you post.
2. Add a **60-second Loom** that explains the app.
3. **Attach the file**, so judges can test the app themselves. These are Jack's words in his pinned comment; he does not say which file. For a Mac app, that is normally the DMG.

## Deadline
Sun Oct 4, 6:00 PM Dubai, which is **10:00 AM ET**. Our target is 9:00 AM ET.

## Other points
- Submissions of decent quality go into the community's shared software collection. The repo stays public and open source.
- Not stated in the post: whether a fork is allowed. Credit Anarlog openly in the post, the Loom and the README.
