# Loom script (60 seconds)

Record at 1440×900 or larger. Close other apps. Have a short Zoom or YouTube clip ready to play as "the other side". Practice once.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Upshot home screen (black, orange "New note") | "This is Upshot: Granola, rebuilt for one person on a Mac. No bot, your notes stay on your Mac, and it works free." |
| 5–15 s | Click **New note**. Play the clip. Point at the You and Them meters | "One click records both sides of any call. Here's me, and here's them. If it can't hear the other side, it tells you right away. Granola doesn't." |
| 15–25 s | Click **Stop**. The summary appears | "Stop, and the newest Claude turns it into clean notes." |
| 25–33 s | Settings › Intelligence › open the model picker. Point at a **New** badge | "The picker updates itself, so it shows models released this week. Use your own key or a free Gemini key." |
| 33–40 s | Back in the note: click a word in the transcript; audio plays | "Unlike Granola, it keeps your audio. Click any word to hear it." |
| 40–50 s | Glaido: hold the key, ask "What did we decide in my last meeting?" | "And it plugs into Glaido. Ask about your meeting from anywhere, and the answer appears at your cursor." |
| 50–57 s | Home: the "Time saved" card | "It even shows the time it's saved you." |
| 57–60 s | Home screen | "Upshot. Built with Claude Code in a weekend, open source, credit to Anarlog." |

**Before recording:** have one finished meeting with a summary, so the home cards show numbers. Connect Glaido first (Settings › Developers › Connect to Glaido).
