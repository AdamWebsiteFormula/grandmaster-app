# Loom script (about 80 seconds)

Record at 1440×900 or larger. Close other apps. Have a short Zoom or YouTube clip ready to play as "the other side". Practice once.

The judges score effectiveness, looks, creativity, simplicity and value. Each beat names the one it serves.

| Time | Show | Say | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | Home: Coming up, notes by day, orange **New note** | "This is Upshot: a Granola-style meeting notepad for Mac, Windows and Linux. No bot joins your call, and it works free the moment you install it." | Value |
| 6–18 s | Press **⌘N**. Point at the timer and the You and Them meters. Keep the clip silent until "No sound from the other side yet" shows (10 seconds; trim the wait in the edit), then play it | "One key records both sides. Here's me, here's them. If it can't hear the other side, it tells you now, not after the meeting. Granola doesn't." | Effectiveness, creativity |
| 18–28 s | Click **Stop**. The summary writes itself | "Stop, and the summary is ready. No key, no account: Upshot AI runs Claude Sonnet 5.5 for free users." | Simplicity, value |
| 28–40 s | Open the transcript. Click a word; the audio plays from there | "Here's the full transcript, by speaker, with times. Click any word to hear that moment. Your recording stays on your computer. Granola keeps none." | Effectiveness |
| 40–48 s | Click **Draft follow-up email** in the bottom bar | "One click drafts the follow-up email." | Value |
| 48–58 s | Glaido: hold the key, ask "What did we decide in my last meeting?" | "And it plugs into Glaido. Ask about any meeting from any app, and the answer appears at your cursor." | Creativity |
| 58–66 s | Settings › Plan › **Upgrade to Pro**. Stripe Checkout with 4242 4242 4242 4242. Back in chat, open **Auto ⌄** | "Pro is real, in Stripe's test mode. Pay with the test card, then pick this week's models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google." | Value |
| 66–76 s | Settings › General: switch Light, Dark, Match my Mac | "Black and orange, light or dark, and it can match your Mac." | Looks |
| 76–80 s | Home | "Upshot. Built with Claude Code in three days, open source, with credit to Anarlog." | |

**Before recording:**
- Settings › Transcription shows **Upshot** / **Nova 3 (cloud)** (Upshot transcription; set on this Mac on Oct 3, and new installs start on it).
- Don't open the old **Welcome to Upshot** note on camera: it was made before tonight and still has the old privacy wording.
- Have one finished meeting with a summary and a transcript, so Home has a note and the transcript has words to click.
- Let the live recording run long enough for a real summary. If Upshot says too little was said, click **Generate summary** and cut the gap.
- Connect Glaido first: Settings › Connectors › Glaido › Create Glaido folder, then in Glaido open Tools › Import and pick that folder.
- For the Pro beat, sign up with a throwaway email before you record, so **Upgrade to Pro** goes straight to Stripe Checkout. Cut the card typing down to about 5 seconds in the edit.
