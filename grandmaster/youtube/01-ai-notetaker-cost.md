## 4. Segments

### Segment 1: The prices side by side

[Title card: "The prices"]

That's the easy part. The real difference is in what you get for free, and what the price leaves out.

### Segment 2: What the free plans really limit

[Title card: "What free plans leave out"]

All four apps have a free plan. But "free" means something different in each one.

Otter's free plan gives you 300 minutes a month. And each conversation stops at 30 minutes. So a one-hour meeting doesn't fit.

Fireflies' free plan gives you 400 minutes of storage for the whole team, and 20 AI credits.

Granola's free plan has no minute limit, but you only see your last 30 days of meetings. After that, older notes are out of reach unless you pay.

Upshot's free plan keeps all of your notes, with no 30-day limit, because they're stored on your own computer. The AI has a fair-use limit per person.

[On screen: the four free plans, one line each.]

So here's the question to ask about any free plan: what happens to my notes after a month, and what happens in a long meeting?

### Segment 3: The hidden costs

[Title card: "The hidden costs"]

There are three costs most people miss.

The first one is monthly versus yearly. Otter Pro is 16.99 a month, but only 8.33 if you pay for the year. That's about half. If you pay monthly, you pay a lot more for the same plan.

The second one is features locked behind a bigger plan. On Granola, integrations like Notion, HubSpot and Zapier, and the API, start on Business. On Otter, a meeting can run up to 90 minutes on Pro, and up to 4 hours on Business, which is 30 dollars a month.

The third one is AI credits. Some apps count how many times you ask the AI a question. Fireflies Pro gives you 20 AI credits. So read what a "credit" is before you buy.

### Segment 4: What it costs for a team

[Title card: "For a team of 10"]
[On screen: one bar per app, yearly total for 10 people.]

Most of these apps charge per person, per month. That adds up fast.

Here's a team of 10 people for one year, on each app's business plan, paid yearly where you can.

Granola Business: 14 dollars times 10 people times 12 months. That's 1,680 dollars a year.

Fireflies Business: 19 dollars a month paid yearly. That's 2,280 dollars a year.

Otter Business: 19.99 a month paid yearly. That's about 2,400 dollars a year.

Upshot doesn't have a team plan today. Each person uses their own free account, so it costs nothing. But you also don't get shared team workspaces or admin controls. If your team needs those, Upshot is not the right fit yet.

### Segment 5: How to choose

[Title card: "How to choose"]

Here's a simple way to choose.

If you have short meetings and only a few a month, a free plan is probably enough. Just check the limit on meeting length.

If you need to find old notes months later, pick a plan with no history limit.

If your notes need to go into a CRM by themselves, plan to pay for a business plan. That feature almost always costs more.

And if you can, pay for the year. It's often much cheaper.
