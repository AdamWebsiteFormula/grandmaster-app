## 4. Segments

### Segment 1: What they have in common

[Title card: "What's the same"]

Let's start with the good news. These two apps work the same way.

No bot joins your call. Nobody sees an extra guest called "Notetaker" in the meeting.

The app records two things on your own computer: your microphone, and the sound of the call.

You type rough notes while you talk. When the meeting ends, the AI turns your notes and the transcript into clean meeting notes.

So if you want a bot-free notetaker, both apps do the main job. The differences are in the details. Let's go through them.

### Segment 2: Price

[Title card: "Price"]
[On screen: a simple price table. Granola left, Upshot right.]

Granola has three plans.

Basic is free. But on Basic, you only see your last 30 days of meetings.

Business is 14 dollars per person per month. That gets you unlimited history, a choice of AI models, and integrations like Notion, HubSpot and Zapier.

Enterprise is 35 dollars per person per month, for big teams with admin controls.

Upshot has two plans.

Free keeps all of your notes, with no 30-day limit. They're stored on your own computer. You sign in with Google or Microsoft, and the AI works right away. There's no key to set up.

Pro is 14 dollars a month, or 11 dollars a month if you pay for the year. Pro adds one thing: you pick the AI model for chat, from the newest models out this week.

So here's the short version. If you want to keep all your notes for free, Upshot is the better deal. If your team needs CRM integrations, that's a Granola Business feature, and Upshot doesn't have it.

[Edit note: Upshot payments are still in test mode. Confirm the Pro price before you publish.]

### Segment 3: Your audio and your transcript

[Title card: "Your audio and your transcript"]

This is the biggest difference between the two apps.

Granola doesn't keep your audio. And you can't edit the transcript.

That sounds small, until the AI gets something wrong. Say the notes show a price of 50,000 dollars, and you think the client said 15. With Granola, you can't go back and listen.

Upshot keeps the recording on your own computer. You click any word in the transcript and hear that exact moment. If a word is wrong, you fix it.

[Screen recording: click a transcript line in Upshot, the audio plays, then edit one word.]

You can also choose how long Upshot keeps your audio, or turn it off.

### Segment 4: Computers and calendars

[Title card: "Computers and calendars"]

Granola runs on Mac and Windows, and it has phone apps.

Upshot runs on Mac, Windows and Linux. If you use Linux, Upshot is the one of these two that works for you.

But Upshot has no phone app today. If you take a lot of meetings on your phone, that matters.

Calendars work differently too. On a Mac, Upshot reads the Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars on your computer, several at the same time. Granola uses the calendar of the account you sign in with.

On Windows and Linux, Upshot has no calendar yet. That's coming next.

### Segment 5: Where Granola wins

[Title card: "Where Granola wins"]

I promised you the honest part. Here it is.

One. Granola is proven. Lots of people use it every day. Upshot is new.

Two. Granola has phone apps. Upshot doesn't, yet.

Three. Granola is built for teams. It has shared workspaces and admin controls, and Upshot doesn't have a team plan today.

Four. Granola connects to tools like HubSpot, Attio, Notion and Zapier, and Salesforce on Enterprise. If your notes need to land in a CRM by themselves, Granola does that today.

If those things are important to you, pick Granola. It's a great app.

### Segment 6: Who should pick which

[Title card: "Which one is right for you?"]

Pick Granola if you work on a team, you need CRM integrations, or you take meetings on your phone.

Pick Upshot if you want all your notes free, with no 30-day limit. If you want to hear your recording and fix your transcript. If you use Linux. Or if you want to pick from the newest AI models the week they come out.
