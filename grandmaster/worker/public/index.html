Upshot

Upshot is a meeting notes app for Mac, Windows and Linux. It records your calls without a bot joining, transcribes them, and writes clear meeting notes with AI. It is free.

Download Upshot

Sign in with Google or Microsoft to turn on Upshot AI and transcription. Your notes stay on your computer.

What Upshot does

  • Records any call, in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack or any other app, with no bot joining.
  • Transcribes as you talk and keeps your audio, so you can click any word to hear it.
  • Writes a summary from the transcript and your own notes, and answers questions about your meetings.
  • Keeps your notes, transcripts and audio on your computer.

Why Upshot asks for your Google or Microsoft account

Upshot uses your name and email address to create your free account, so Upshot AI and transcription know who is asking and can apply a fair limit per person. If you choose to connect your calendar, Upshot reads your events to name your meeting notes and remind you before meetings. It never changes your calendar. Read the privacy policy for the details.
