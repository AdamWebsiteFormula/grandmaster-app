Recording and consent

Upshot records the meetings you start it in. Laws about recording differ by place. Some places, such as California, need every person on the call to agree.

  • You are responsible for telling people that you record and transcribe, and for getting their consent where the law needs it.
  • Don't record a meeting when someone says no.
  • On Mac and Linux, Upshot can post a short notice in the meeting chat when recording starts. Turn on "Post a short notice in the meeting chat" in Settings › Meetings. The notice tells people; it does not get their consent for you.
